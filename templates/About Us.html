About Us

We are a team dedicated to providing seamless proposal management solutions. Our platform allows users to upload, manage, and search for proposals with ease.

Welcome to PropMan

PropMan is a smart, secure, and efficient platform for managing proposals in both academic and professional environments.

Whether you're part of a university research department submitting grant proposals or a corporate team handling client RFPs, PropMan simplifies and centralizes your entire proposal workflow.

Our Purpose

PropMan was created to eliminate the complexity and fragmentation in traditional proposal processes. We bring everything — from drafting and collaboration to submission and archiving — under one intuitive platform.

Who We Serve

🎓 Academic & Research Institutions

  • Submit, store, and retrieve research proposals
  • Track submissions by department, funding agency, or research field
  • Ensure compliance with grant guidelines and institutional standards

🏢 Corporate & Industry Teams

  • Manage business proposals, RFPs, tenders, and technical documents
  • Collaborate across departments with version control
  • Search, filter, and audit historical submissions for insights and compliance

Key Features

  • 🔍 Advanced Search: Quickly find proposals by title, standard, industry, or keywords
  • 📂 Secure Document Upload: Centralize files with organized metadata
  • 👥 User Roles: Control access with secure login and role-based permissions
  • 🕒 Activity Tracking: Know who submitted what and when
  • 📈 Scalable: Designed to grow with your organization

Our Mission

To empower academic and corporate teams with the tools they need to manage proposal lifecycles with clarity, control, and confidence.
